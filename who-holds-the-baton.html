Picking one

"Answer this" or "handle this"

Reach for chat
  • You want to think, not delegate.
  • The next question depends on this answer.
  • You would have to check every step anyway.
Reach for an agent
  • The goal is clear and you can describe "done".
  • It is several steps of legwork you already understand.
  • You would rather review a result than steer a process.

The catch. The agent ran five steps of work before checking in. That is the point, and the risk: if step two went wrong, you find out five steps later. The same trade as delegating to a person — so keep goals small enough to describe, and let anything that leaves the building stop and ask.
